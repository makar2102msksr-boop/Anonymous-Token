FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

What is Anonymous Token?

Anonymous Token is a community-driven token designed for the Solana ecosystem.

What blockchain is used?

Anonymous Token uses the Solana network for its payment and ecosystem infrastructure.

How do I participate?

Connect a compatible wallet, enter the amount of SOL you want to contribute and review the transaction in your wallet before approving it.

Which wallets are supported?

Phantom is supported through its Solana provider. Trust Wallet and MetaMask are available when their current environment exposes a compatible Solana provider.

When will I receive $ANON?

During this initial version, token distribution is handled manually after the payment transaction has been verified on-chain.
