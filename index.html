FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

What is the presale rate?

The configured presale rate is 400,000 $ANON for every 1 SOL.

How large is the presale?

4 billion $ANON, equal to 40% of the 10 billion total supply, are allocated to the presale.

What is the presale target?

The full presale allocation corresponds to a target of 10,000 SOL.

How is progress calculated?

The progress bar uses the global amount returned by the presale statistics API. 10,000 SOL equals 100% of the presale.

Which wallets are supported?

Phantom works when its Solana provider is available. Trust Wallet and MetaMask are detected only when the wallet environment exposes a compatible Solana provider.

When will I receive $ANON?

In this version payment is verified on-chain and token distribution is performed manually.
